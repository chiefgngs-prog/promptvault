Free ChatGPT Prompts for Finance Professionals

You close the books, build the models, and explain the numbers to people who want the answer in one sentence. Month-end never ends, the auditors always want one more schedule, and turning a dense spreadsheet into a clean summary for leadership takes longer than building the spreadsheet did. ChatGPT cannot do your reconciliations or guarantee the math, but it excels at the writing wrapped around the numbers — drafting a CFO-ready summary, translating jargon for a non-finance audience, explaining a variance in plain English, or shaping a budget narrative from your bullet points. The risk is obvious in finance: the model will invent figures if you let it, so these prompts are built to draft structure and language from numbers you provide, never to generate the numbers themselves. Fill in your figures and context, review every output against your source data, and let it handle the prose. Choose the prompt that fits the task below.

13 Free Prompts

Budget Builder 50/30/20

Help me create a monthly budget using the 50/30/20 method. Income: [AMOUNT]. Fixed expenses: [LIST]. Categorize spending into Needs (50%), Wants (30%), and Savings/Debt (20%). Identify where I'm over/under budget, suggest specific cuts, create a tracking system, and set up sinking funds for irregular expenses.

Debt Payoff Strategy

I have these debts: [LIST WITH BALANCES, INTEREST RATES, MINIMUM PAYMENTS]. I can pay [AMOUNT] monthly total. Compare snowball vs avalanche methods. Create a month-by-month payoff timeline for each. Calculate total interest paid and time to debt-free for both. Recommend the best method for my psychology and math, with reasoning.

Emergency Fund Planner

Help me build an emergency fund. Current savings: [AMOUNT]. Monthly expenses: [AMOUNT]. Target: [3/6/12] months. Create: monthly savings target, timeline to fully funded, where to park the money (HYSA vs money market vs CD ladder), automation setup, what qualifies as an emergency (and what doesn't), and rebuild plan after using it.

Investment Portfolio Builder

I'm [AGE], risk tolerance [CONSERVATIVE/MODERATE/AGGRESSIVE], investing for [GOAL] in [TIMEFRAME]. Recommend an asset allocation: stocks/bonds/cash/alternatives percentages. Within stocks: domestic/international/large-cap/small-cap. Suggest specific low-cost index funds or ETFs for each slice. Include rebalancing schedule and contribution strategy.

Retirement Calculator Guide

I'm [AGE], want to retire at [AGE], currently have [AMOUNT] saved, saving [AMOUNT]/month. Estimate: will I hit my target? Run scenarios for different return rates (6%, 8%, 10%). Calculate the 4% rule withdrawal amount. Identify if I'm on track, ahead, or behind. Suggest specific changes to close any gap. Consider inflation.

Side Income Optimizer

I earn [AMOUNT] from my [JOB/SIDE HUSTLE]. Analyze: current effective tax rate, opportunities for tax-advantaged accounts (IRA, 401k, HSA), deductible expenses I'm missing, business structure considerations (LLC vs sole proprietor), quarterly tax estimation, and audit-proof recordkeeping system. Suggest 3 strategies to legally reduce tax burden.

House vs Rent Decision

I'm deciding whether to buy or rent in [CITY]. Home price: [AMOUNT]. Rent equivalent: [AMOUNT]. Down payment available: [AMOUNT]. Run the 5-year cost comparison: total cost of buying (down payment, mortgage, property tax, insurance, maintenance, HOA, closing costs) vs renting (rent + renter's insurance + opportunity cost of invested down payment).

Subscription Audit

Help me audit my subscriptions: [LIST WITH MONTHLY COSTS]. For each: evaluate usage frequency, cost per use, cheaper alternatives, duplicate services, and emotional attachment. Calculate total annual cost. Categorize: keep, downgrade, cancel. Estimate annual savings from recommended cuts. Create a system for ongoing subscription management.

Financial Independence Calculator

Calculate my path to FIRE (Financial Independence, Retire Early). Current age: [X], annual expenses: [X], current net worth: [X], annual savings rate: [X]%. Calculate: my FIRE number (25x annual expenses), years to FIRE at current savings rate, how accelerating savings by [X]% changes the timeline, and coast FIRE / lean FIRE / fat FIRE scenarios.

Expense Tracking System

Design an expense tracking system that I'll actually stick with. Consider: my style ([SPREADSHEET/APP/ENVELOPE]), categories relevant to my life, automation opportunities (bank API, receipt scanning), weekly review ritual, monthly close-out process, and annual summary for tax purposes. Include a starter spreadsheet template description with formulas.

First-Time Home Buyer Guide

I'm a first-time home buyer in [COUNTRY/STATE]. Create a step-by-step guide: financial preparation (credit score target, debt-to-income ratio, down payment), mortgage types and how to choose, pre-approval process, house hunting criteria, offer strategy, inspection and closing process, hidden costs, and first-year homeownership financial planning.

College Savings Planner

Help me plan for my [AGE]-year-old child's college education. Current savings: [AMOUNT]. Target school type: [PUBLIC/PRIVATE]. Create: estimated cost at college age (with inflation), monthly savings needed, account types (529, Coverdell, custodial), investment strategy within the account, and what-if scenarios for scholarships, in-state, or trade school.

Tax Deduction Finder

Based on my situation ([EMPLOYMENT STATUS, FILING STATUS, STATE]), identify all tax deductions and credits I likely qualify for. Consider: standard vs itemized, education, home office, self-employment, healthcare, charitable, retirement contributions, and energy credits. Estimate total tax savings. List documentation needed. Flag commonly missed deductions.
