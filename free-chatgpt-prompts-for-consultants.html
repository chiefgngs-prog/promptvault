Free ChatGPT Prompts for Consultants

Your clients pay for clarity. They hand you a mess of a problem, and they expect a structured analysis, a sharp recommendation, and a deck that makes the answer obvious to their board. The thinking is yours, but the packaging — the proposal, the interview guide, the status update, the executive summary — eats hours you would rather spend on the actual work. ChatGPT is useful precisely here, as a first-draft partner for the structure around your insight. It can turn rough notes into a proposal outline, suggest a framework for a fuzzy problem, draft interview questions for a discovery session, or compress a long findings document into a one-page summary a busy executive will actually read. These prompts are built to leverage your expertise, not replace it — you supply the context and judgment, the model supplies the scaffolding and the prose. Add your details, refine the output, and deliver.

13 Free Prompts

Client Discovery Questionnaire

Design a discovery questionnaire for a consulting engagement with a [INDUSTRY] client seeking help with [PROBLEM AREA]. Create 20 questions covering: current state, pain points, goals, success metrics, constraints, stakeholders, timeline, budget, previous attempts, competitive landscape, and decision-making process. Order from broad to specific.

Consulting Proposal Writer

Write a consulting proposal for [CLIENT TYPE] experiencing [PROBLEM]. Include: executive summary, situation assessment, proposed approach (phases with deliverables), methodology, timeline with milestones, team composition and roles, investment and payment terms, assumptions and exclusions, success metrics, and next steps. Tone: confident and professional.

SWOT Facilitation Guide

Create a facilitation guide for leading a SWOT workshop with [CLIENT TYPE] leadership team. Include: pre-work for participants, 90-minute agenda with time blocks, ground rules, probing questions for each SWOT category, prioritization exercise, TOWS matrix (turn weaknesses into strengths, threats into opportunities), and deliverable template.

Process Improvement Plan

I'm consulting for a [COMPANY TYPE] with this problem: [DESCRIBE]. Apply Lean/Six Sigma principles. Create: current state process map description, waste identification (8 wastes), root cause analysis (5 Whys + Fishbone), future state design, improvement initiatives (ranked by impact/effort), implementation roadmap, and KPIs to track.

Change Management Framework

Create a change management plan for [ORGANIZATION] implementing [CHANGE]. Use Kotter's 8-step or ADKAR model. For each step: specific actions, timeline, stakeholders to engage, communication plan, resistance mitigation strategies, training needs, quick wins to build momentum, and success indicators. Include a stakeholder influence map.

Market Sizing Model

Help me size the market for [PRODUCT/SERVICE] in [GEOGRAPHY]. Use both top-down and bottom-up approaches. Top-down: TAM, SAM, SOM with sources. Bottom-up: number of potential customers x average spend. Include: market growth rate, trends affecting size, assumptions and confidence levels, data source recommendations, and sensitivity analysis.

Stakeholder Analysis Matrix

Create a stakeholder analysis for [PROJECT/CHANGE] at [ORGANIZATION]. List likely stakeholders. For each: assess interest level (1-5), influence level (1-5), current position (supporter/neutral/resister), what they care about, key message for them, engagement strategy (manage closely, keep satisfied, keep informed, monitor), and influence plan.

Business Case Builder

Build a business case for [INVESTMENT/INITIATIVE] at [COMPANY TYPE]. Include: problem statement, proposed solution, cost analysis (CapEx + OpEx over 3-5 years), benefit quantification (revenue increase + cost savings + risk reduction), ROI calculation, payback period, NPV, sensitivity analysis, risks and mitigations, alternatives considered, and recommendation.

Operating Model Design

Design a target operating model for [ORGANIZATION] post-[CHANGE/MERGER/TRANSFORMATION]. Define: value streams, organizational structure, governance model, processes (to-be), technology stack, people and capabilities needed, location strategy, performance metrics, and transition plan from current to future state. Create a one-page visual summary.

Consulting Engagement Kickoff

Create a kickoff agenda and materials for a consulting engagement with [CLIENT]. Include: 90-minute agenda, objectives for the meeting, introduction script, project overview presentation outline, roles and responsibilities (RACI), communication plan, governance cadence, risk register template, and action item template. Set the tone for partnership.

Digital Transformation Roadmap

Create a digital transformation roadmap for [INDUSTRY/COMPANY TYPE]. Current maturity: [DESCRIBE]. Define: target digital maturity across dimensions (customer experience, operations, culture, technology), initiatives sequenced by priority, dependencies, quick wins vs strategic bets, investment requirements, change management needs, and 3-year milestone plan.

Vendor Selection Framework

Create a vendor selection framework for [CATEGORY OF SPEND]. Define: requirements (must-have and nice-to-have), evaluation criteria with weights, scoring methodology, RFP structure, vendor longlist strategy, demo evaluation guide, reference check questions, total cost of ownership model, negotiation levers, and recommendation template.

M&A Integration Plan

Create a post-merger integration plan for [ACQUIRING COMPANY] acquiring [TARGET COMPANY]. Define: integration approach (absorption/preservation/best-of-both), workstreams (culture, operations, IT, HR, finance, customer), Day 1 checklist, 30/60/90-day milestones, synergy targets and tracking, governance structure, communication plan, and risk mitigation.
