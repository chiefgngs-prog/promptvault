Free ChatGPT Prompts for Etsy Sellers

Etsy rewards clarity and punishes vague listings. If your item is buried on page seven, it's usually the title, the tags, or a description that reads like a spec sheet instead of a reason to buy. Most sellers know their craft cold but stall when it's time to write — the SEO-friendly title that still sounds human, the thirteen tags that actually match how people search, the first line that stops a scroll. Then there's the rest: shop announcements, message-to-buyer templates, social posts to drive traffic, and restock updates that feel personal instead of copy-pasted. These 13 free ChatGPT prompts handle the writing that sits between making and selling. Use them to draft listing descriptions, build tag clusters around real search terms, write holiday and sale announcements, and respond to tricky buyer messages without overthinking each word. They're starting points, not magic — you bring the product knowledge, the prompts handle the phrasing. Copy them below, tweak the details, and reuse them across your whole shop.

13 Free Prompts

Product Launch Strategy

Create a complete product launch strategy for [PRODUCT] in [MARKET]. Include: pre-launch (teasers, waitlist, early bird), launch day (announcement, limited-time offer, live event), post-launch (social proof, follow-up, upsell). Timeline: 30 days.

Amazon Listing Optimizer

Optimize an Amazon product listing for [PRODUCT]. Include: SEO-optimized title, 5 bullet points (benefit-focused), product description, backend search terms, A+ content suggestions, and review strategy. Target keywords: [KEYWORDS].

Shopify Store Audit

Create a comprehensive audit checklist for a Shopify store selling [PRODUCTS]. Check: homepage design, product pages, navigation, checkout process, mobile optimization, site speed, trust signals, SEO, and email capture. Provide 20 prioritized improvements.

Abandoned Cart Recovery

Design an abandoned cart recovery sequence for [STORE TYPE]. Include: 3-email sequence (timing: 1hr, 24hr, 72hr), subject lines, email copy, incentive strategy (discount vs free shipping), SMS option, and success metrics. Recover rate target: [TARGET].

Product Bundling Strategy

Create a product bundling strategy for [PRODUCT LINE]. Analyze: which products to bundle, pricing psychology, bundle naming, presentation on website, A/B testing plan, and expected impact on AOV (Average Order Value).

Customer Loyalty Program

Design a customer loyalty program for [E-COMMERCE STORE]. Include: program structure (points/tiers/VIP), reward catalog, earning mechanics, redemption process, promotional strategy, technology platform, and success metrics.

Black Friday Strategy

Create a Black Friday/Cyber Monday strategy for [STORE]. Include: discount strategy, doorbuster deals, email campaign timeline (2 weeks), social media plan, landing page design, inventory prep, staffing plan, and post-BFCM retention.

Dropshipping Product Researcher

Identify 20 potential dropshipping products in [NICHE]. For each: product name, estimated cost, suggested retail price, profit margin, target audience, marketing angle, competition level, and trend trajectory. Focus on problem-solving products.

Subscription Box Designer

Design a subscription box concept for [NICHE/THEME]. Include: box concept and name, target audience, contents (month 1 sample), pricing strategy, sourcing plan, packaging design, unboxing experience, and churn reduction strategies.

E-commerce SEO Strategy

Create an e-commerce SEO strategy for [STORE]. Include: keyword research approach, category page optimization, product page template, blog content strategy (10 topics), technical SEO checklist, link building plan, and tracking dashboard.

Returns & Refunds Policy Writer

Write a customer-friendly returns and refunds policy for [STORE TYPE]. Include: return window, conditions, process steps, refund methods, exchange options, restocking fees, and FAQs. Balance customer satisfaction with business protection.

Influencer Gift Guide Strategy

Create an influencer gifting strategy for [PRODUCT]. Include: target influencer criteria, outreach template, gift package design, unboxing experience, follow-up sequence, content guidelines, usage rights, and tracking method.

Wholesale Pricing Sheet

Create a wholesale pricing structure for [PRODUCT LINE]. Include: retail prices, wholesale prices (with margins), minimum order quantities, volume discounts, payment terms, shipping policies, and a one-page wholesale line sheet template.
