Free ChatGPT Prompts for Teachers

You plan lessons, grade stacks of work, email parents, differentiate for a room full of different learners, and still have to write sub plans when you are out sick. The planning never stops, and the unpaid hours stack up faster than the to-do list shrinks. ChatGPT will not replace your judgment in the classroom, but it can take the first pass off your plate — drafting a lesson outline, building a quiz from your objectives, turning a rough rubric into a clean scoring guide, or rewriting the same science explanation for three different reading levels. The catch is knowing what to ask. Generic prompts give you generic output you still have to fix. These prompts are written for real classroom tasks: lesson hooks, exit tickets, progress-report comments, accommodation suggestions, parent messages that sound professional without sounding cold, and activity ideas when your energy is low. Copy any prompt, fill in the bracketed details about your grade and subject, and paste the result into whatever you use. Grab the ones you need below.

13 Free Prompts

Lesson Plan Generator

Act as an experienced teacher. Create a detailed 60-minute lesson plan for [GRADE LEVEL] students on [TOPIC]. Include: learning objectives, materials needed, introduction/hook (5 min), main activity (30 min), group work (15 min), assessment/exit ticket (10 min), and homework assignment. Differentiate for visual, auditory, and kinesthetic learners.

Complex Topic Explainer

Explain [COMPLEX TOPIC] as if you were teaching it to a [GRADE/LEVEL] student. Use the Feynman Technique: break it down into simple language, use real-world analogies, create a step-by-step explanation, identify common points of confusion, and provide 3 self-check questions to verify understanding.

Quiz Question Generator

Generate 20 quiz questions about [TOPIC] at [DIFFICULTY] level. Include: 5 multiple choice, 5 true/false, 5 fill-in-the-blank, 3 short answer, and 2 essay questions. Provide the correct answer and a brief explanation for each. Make questions test different cognitive levels (recall, understanding, application, analysis).

Presentation Builder

Create a [LENGTH]-slide presentation outline on [TOPIC] for [AUDIENCE]. For each slide include: slide title, bullet points (max 5), speaker notes, suggested visual/image, and transition to next slide. Start with a compelling hook and end with a memorable call-to-action or takeaway.

Concept Map Generator

Create a text-based concept map for [TOPIC]. Start with the central concept, then branch out to 5 major sub-concepts. For each sub-concept, provide 3-4 related details or examples. Show relationships between concepts using arrows and connection words (leads to, depends on, is part of, contrasts with).

Debate Argument Preparer

Prepare arguments for a debate on [TOPIC] taking the [PRO/CON] position. Provide: opening statement (2 paragraphs), 5 main arguments each with evidence and reasoning, anticipated counterarguments with rebuttals, powerful quotes or statistics, and a closing statement. Keep arguments logical and persuasive.

Science Fair Project Planner

Design a winning science fair project for a [GRADE] student interested in [FIELD]. Include: project title, testable question, hypothesis, materials list (household items), step-by-step procedure, variables (independent, dependent, control), data collection method, display board layout, and tips for judge interviews.

Historical Timeline Builder

Create a detailed timeline of [HISTORICAL EVENT/ERA]. Include 15-20 key events in chronological order, each with: date, event description, cause, effect, historical significance, and key figures involved. Group events into phases and explain how each phase led to the next.

Reading Comprehension Guide

Create a reading comprehension guide for [BOOK/ARTICLE TITLE]. Include: pre-reading questions to activate prior knowledge, vocabulary to preview, chapter-by-chapter discussion questions (literal, inferential, and evaluative), literary devices to identify, themes to track, and a post-reading reflection activity.

Group Project Organizer

Create a project management plan for a group school project on [TOPIC] with [NUMBER] team members over [TIMELINE]. Include: task breakdown with assignments, deadlines for each milestone, meeting schedule, communication plan, quality standards, conflict resolution process, and peer evaluation rubric.

Critical Thinking Exercise Builder

Design 10 critical thinking exercises based on [TOPIC]. Each exercise should require: analyzing information, questioning assumptions, evaluating evidence, identifying biases, synthesizing multiple viewpoints, and drawing reasoned conclusions. Vary the format: scenarios, data analysis, ethical dilemmas, and logic puzzles.

Rubric Designer

Create a detailed grading rubric for a [ASSIGNMENT TYPE] in [SUBJECT]. Define 5 criteria (e.g., content, organization, creativity, mechanics, citation). For each criterion, describe what Exemplary (4), Proficient (3), Developing (2), and Beginning (1) looks like. Include point values and overall grade calculation.

Differentiated Instruction Planner

Create a differentiated instruction plan for a lesson on [TOPIC] for a class with diverse learners. Differentiate by: readiness (3 tiers of difficulty), interest (choice boards with 4 options), and learning profile (visual, auditory, kinesthetic activities). For each differentiation: describe the activity, materials needed, how you'll assess, and grouping strategy. Include management tips for running stations.
