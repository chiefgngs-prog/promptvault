How to Write ChatGPT Prompts That Actually Work

Prompt Engineering Guide | Updated 2025 | 8 min read

Most people type a sentence into ChatGPT and get garbage back. Then they blame the AI. The problem isn't ChatGPT. It's the prompt.

The 5-Element Framework

Every professional prompt includes these 5 elements:

  • 1. Role Assignment - Tell the AI who to be
  • 2. Context - Give background information
  • 3. Task Specification - Be specific about what you want
  • 4. Output Format - Tell it how to structure the response
  • 5. Quality Constraints - Set standards

Bad vs Good Prompts

Bad Prompt

Write me a blog post about marketing.

This gives you generic, boring, useless content.

Good Prompt

Act as a content marketing expert. Write a 1500-word blog post about [TOPIC] for [AUDIENCE]. Structure it with: an engaging hook, 3 main sections with actionable advice, real-world examples, and a strong CTA. Use a professional but conversational tone. Include relevant keywords: [KEYWORDS]. Avoid fluff and generic statements.

This gives you a polished, ready-to-publish article.

The Difference Is Structure

Notice how the good prompt includes all 5 elements:

  • Role: "Act as a content marketing expert"
  • Context: "[TOPIC] for [AUDIENCE]"
  • Task: "Write a 1500-word blog post"
  • Format: "engaging hook, 3 main sections, examples, CTA"
  • Constraints: "professional but conversational, avoid fluff"

Before and After Examples

Ad Copy

BAD: Write a Facebook ad for my product
GOOD: Act as a direct response copywriter. Write 10 Facebook ad variations for [PRODUCT] targeting [AUDIENCE]. Use 3 hook styles (question, bold statement, story), 2 body copy variations, clear CTAs. Optimize for high CTR.

Email

BAD: Write a sales email
GOOD: Write a 5-email cold outreach sequence for [PRODUCT] targeting [ICP]. Include subject lines, personalized openers, value prop, social proof, soft CTA, follow-up timing, breakup email. Keep each under 150 words.

SEO

BAD: Help me with SEO
GOOD: Create a keyword research strategy for [WEBSITE]. Include: seed keywords (20), long-tail (30), question-based (15), commercial (10), keyword clustering, search intent mapping, and prioritization framework.
