50 Best ChatGPT Prompts for Business Owners in 2026

Updated July 2026 · 12 min read · By PromptVault Team

Running a business means wearing too many hats. ChatGPT can be your strategy consultant, copywriter, financial analyst, and brainstorming partner — if you know how to ask. Below are 50 battle-tested prompts that real business owners use to save hours every week.

Strategy & Planning

1. Business Model Canvas Generator

Act as a business strategist. Create a complete Business Model Canvas for [BUSINESS IDEA]. Include all 9 sections with specific, actionable details for each.

2. SWOT Analysis Deep Dive

Conduct a comprehensive SWOT analysis for [COMPANY]. For each category, provide 5 detailed points with explanations. Then suggest 3 strategic actions.

3. Competitor Analysis Framework

Analyze [MY COMPANY] vs [TOP 3 COMPETITORS]. Compare pricing, features, marketing strategy, strengths, weaknesses. Output as a comparison table.

4. Pricing Strategy Optimizer

Help me develop a pricing strategy for [PRODUCT]. Consider production costs, competitor pricing, perceived value, and market positioning. Provide 3 tier options.

5. Investor Pitch Deck Outline

Create a compelling 12-slide pitch deck outline for [STARTUP]. For each slide, provide key talking points and suggested visuals.

6. OKR Framework Builder

Create quarterly OKRs for [DEPARTMENT]. Provide 3 objectives, each with 3-4 measurable key results. Make them ambitious yet achievable.

7. Market Entry Strategy

I want to enter [MARKET] in [REGION]. Analyze market size, barriers, competitive landscape, and recommend the best entry mode. Provide a 90-day plan.

8. Scalability Assessment

Evaluate the scalability of [BUSINESS MODEL]. Analyze bottlenecks, infrastructure needs, and provide a scaling roadmap for 2x, 5x, and 10x growth.

Marketing & Growth

9. Marketing Calendar Builder

Create a 3-month marketing calendar for [BUSINESS]. Include content themes, campaign ideas, social media posts, email campaigns, and budget allocation per month.

10. Customer Persona Generator

Create 3 detailed customer personas for [PRODUCT]. For each: name, occupation, goals, pain points, buying triggers, and a quote capturing their mindset.

11. Brand Positioning Statement

Write a brand positioning statement for [BRAND] targeting [AUDIENCE] who need [PROBLEM SOLVED]. Include frame of reference, point of difference, and reason to believe.

12. Viral Content Brainstormer

Generate 15 viral content ideas for [BRAND] on [PLATFORM]. For each: hook, format, why it could go viral, and production requirements.

13. SEO Keyword Strategy

Create an SEO keyword strategy for [WEBSITE] in [INDUSTRY]. Identify: 10 primary keywords, 20 long-tail opportunities, content gaps vs competitors, and a 3-month plan.

Sales & Revenue

14. Sales Script Generator

Write a sales script for [PRODUCT] targeting [AUDIENCE]. Structure: opener, problem agitation, solution presentation, objection handling, and close. Make it conversational.

15. Cold Email Sequence

Write a 5-email cold sequence for [PRODUCT] targeting [ICP]. Email 1: pattern interrupt + value. Email 2: case study. Email 3: insight. Email 4: different angle. Email 5: breakup.

16. Objection Handler

Create an objection handling playbook for [PRODUCT]. For the top 10 objections, provide: acknowledge/reframe technique, probing questions, and redirect to next step.

17. Pricing Objection Script

A prospect says 'it's too expensive.' Write a response that: validates their concern, reframes value, anchors against cost of inaction, and proposes options.

Copywriting

18. Landing Page Copy

Write landing page copy for [PRODUCT] using the PAS framework. Sections: hero headline + subhead, problem, agitation, solution, social proof, features/benefits, CTA.

19. Ad Copy Generator

Write 10 ad variations for [PRODUCT] on [PLATFORM]. Mix emotional angles: fear of missing out, aspiration, social proof, curiosity, and logic. Include headline, body, CTA.

20. Email Subject Line Optimizer

Generate 20 email subject lines for [CAMPAIGN]. Mix curiosity, urgency, benefit, personalization, and question formats. Rate each for open rate likelihood.

Operations & Finance

21. KPI Dashboard Designer

Design a KPI dashboard for [BUSINESS TYPE]. Identify the top 10 KPIs categorized by financial, customer, operational, and growth metrics. Define targets and data sources.

22. Cost Reduction Strategy

Act as a CFO. Identify cost reduction opportunities for [BUSINESS TYPE] across operations, technology, marketing, staffing. Estimate annual savings per suggestion.

23. Vendor Negotiation Script

Help me negotiate a better rate with [VENDOR TYPE]. Provide: research checklist, opening offer strategy, concessions to trade, and scripts for the conversation.

Team & Leadership

24. Job Description Writer

Write a compelling job description for [ROLE] at [COMPANY]. Include company overview, responsibilities, requirements, and what you offer. Make it sell the role.

25. Performance Review Template

Create a 360-degree performance review template. Include self-assessment, peer feedback, manager assessment, goal review, and development areas.

26. Meeting Agenda Optimizer

I have a [TYPE] meeting with [NUMBER] people for [DURATION]. Create an agenda that: respects time, drives decisions, assigns accountability, and includes pre-reading.

27. Conflict Resolution Script

I'm mediating a conflict between [TWO EMPLOYEES]. Provide a script for the conversation using nonviolent communication. Include de-escalation techniques.

Innovation & Problem Solving

28. Revenue Stream Brainstormer

Generate 15 creative revenue stream ideas for [BUSINESS TYPE]. For each: implementation difficulty, time to revenue, and potential monthly income. Rank by ROI.

29. Product Feature Prioritization

I have these feature ideas: [LIST]. Help me prioritize using the RICE framework (Reach, Impact, Confidence, Effort). Score each and recommend the top 3 to build next.

30. Lean Experiment Designer

Design a lean startup experiment to validate [HYPOTHESIS]. Define minimum viable test, success criteria, metrics, timeline, budget, and what to do based on results.

Productivity

31. Inbox Zero System

Design an email management system that gets me to inbox zero daily. Include: filtering rules, response templates, batching schedule, and escalation criteria.

32. Weekly Review Checklist

Create a 30-minute weekly review checklist covering: achievements, metrics, lessons learned, next week priorities, relationship maintenance, and personal energy.

Customer Experience

33. NPS Follow-Up Generator

Write NPS follow-up messages for promoters (9-10), passives (7-8), and detractors (0-6). Each should: acknowledge their score, ask a specific question, and include a next step.

34. Customer Journey Mapper

Map the customer journey for [PRODUCT]. For each stage (awareness, consideration, purchase, onboarding, usage, advocacy): touchpoints, emotions, pain points, and improvement opportunities.
