How to Use ChatGPT for Marketing: 30 Prompts That Actually Work

Updated July 2026 · By PromptVault Team

ChatGPT isn't replacing marketers — it's making them 10x faster. The key is knowing what to ask. Here are 30 prompts that real marketing teams use to write content, plan campaigns, optimize SEO, and create ads in minutes instead of hours.

Content Marketing Prompts

Content is still king, but ChatGPT helps you produce more of it faster. These prompts help with ideation, drafting, and optimization.

Content Calendar Generator

Create a 4-week content calendar for [BRAND] targeting [AUDIENCE]. Include blog posts, social media content, email campaigns, and video ideas. Map each to a customer journey stage.

Blog Post Outline

Create a detailed outline for a blog post titled '[TOPIC]'. Include: hook, problem, solution (3-5 sections), examples, FAQ, and CTA. Optimize for SEO keyword: [KEYWORD].

Content Repurposer

I have this blog post: [PASTE]. Create: 5 Twitter posts, 3 LinkedIn posts, 1 Instagram carousel outline, 1 email newsletter excerpt, and 1 YouTube video script.

Social Media Prompts

Social media demands constant content. These prompts help you create platform-native content that actually engages.

Viral Hook Generator

Generate 20 scroll-stopping hooks for [PLATFORM] about [TOPIC]. Each hook should: create curiosity, promise value, or challenge a belief. No clickbait.

Platform Content Adapter

Adapt this content for Instagram (carousel), TikTok (15s video script), LinkedIn (professional post), Twitter (7-tweet thread): [CONTENT]

Community Response Templates

Create response templates for: positive comments, negative comments, questions, complaints, and troll comments on [PLATFORM]. Keep brand voice [DESCRIBE].

Email Marketing Prompts

Email has the highest ROI of any marketing channel. These prompts improve your open rates, click rates, and conversions.

Welcome Email Sequence

Write a 5-email welcome sequence for new subscribers to [NEWSLETTER/BRAND]. Email 1: welcome + freebie. Email 2: story. Email 3: best content. Email 4: social proof. Email 5: soft pitch.

Subject Line A/B Test

Write 10 subject line pairs for [EMAIL TYPE] that test: curiosity vs benefit, short vs long, question vs statement, emoji vs no emoji. Explain the psychology of each.

Re-engagement Campaign

Write a 3-email re-engagement campaign for inactive subscribers. Email 1: we miss you. Email 2: here's what's new. Email 3: last chance before we say goodbye.

SEO Prompts

SEO is a long game, but ChatGPT can accelerate keyword research, content optimization, and technical SEO.

Keyword Cluster Builder

Given the seed keyword [KEYWORD], generate 50 related long-tail keywords. Group them into 5 topic clusters. For each cluster, suggest one pillar article and 4 supporting articles.

Meta Description Writer

Write 5 meta description variations for a page about [TOPIC]. Each should: be under 155 characters, include [KEYWORD], have a CTA, and entice the click.

FAQ Schema Generator

Create 10 FAQ questions and answers for [TOPIC] that I can use as FAQ schema markup. Questions should target featured snippet opportunities.

Advertising Prompts

Write better ad copy, test more angles, and optimize your campaigns with these prompts.

Facebook Ad Copy Generator

Write 5 Facebook ad variations for [PRODUCT] targeting [AUDIENCE]. Each should use a different angle: social proof, fear of missing out, aspiration, transformation, and logic.

Google Ad Copy Writer

Write 10 Google Ads headlines (30 chars max) and 5 descriptions (90 chars max) for [PRODUCT/SERVICE]. Target keyword: [KEYWORD]. Include a strong CTA.

Ad Creative Brief

Write a creative brief for a [PLATFORM] ad campaign for [PRODUCT]. Include: objective, audience, key message, tone, visual direction, and success metrics.
