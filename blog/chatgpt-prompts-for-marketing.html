Facebook and Social Media Ad Prompts

1. Facebook Ad Copy Generator

Prompt: Write 10 variations of Facebook ad copy for [PRODUCT]. Target audience: [DESCRIPTION]. Include: 3 hook styles (question, bold statement, story), 2 body copy variations, clear CTAs, and suggested emoji usage. Optimize for high CTR.

2. Retargeting Campaign Builder

Prompt: Create a retargeting campaign strategy for [WEBSITE]. Define: audience segments (visitors, cart abandoners, past customers), ad creative for each segment, frequency caps, budget allocation, platform recommendations, and expected ROI.

3. TikTok Strategy Builder

Prompt: Create a TikTok marketing strategy for [BRAND]. Include: content pillars, trending audio strategy, posting frequency, video format ideas (10), hashtag strategy, creator collaboration approach, and growth milestones.

Content Marketing Prompts

4. Content Calendar Creator

Prompt: Create a 30-day content calendar for [BRAND] on [PLATFORM]. Include: daily post topics, content types (video, carousel, story, blog), captions, hashtag sets, best posting times, and content themes. Align with [MARKETING GOAL].

5. Blog Post Outline Generator

Prompt: Create a comprehensive blog post outline for [TOPIC]. Target keyword: [KEYWORD]. Include: compelling title options (5), meta description, H2/H3 structure, key points for each section, internal linking opportunities, and a strong CTA.

6. Viral Content Blueprint

Prompt: Generate 10 viral content ideas for [BRAND] on [PLATFORM]. For each idea, explain: the hook, why it would go viral, format, estimated production time, and a draft of the content. Focus on shareability and emotional triggers.

Email Marketing Prompts

7. Welcome Email Sequence

Prompt: Write a 5-email welcome sequence for [BRAND]. Include: Email 1 (Welcome + deliver lead magnet), Email 2 (Brand story), Email 3 (Best content/resources), Email 4 (Social proof), Email 5 (Soft pitch). Subject lines for each.

8. Product Launch Email Sequence

Prompt: Write a 7-email product launch sequence for [PRODUCT]. Include: teaser, education, feature deep dive, social proof, FAQ/objection handling, cart open with early bird, last chance urgency. Subject lines for each.

SEO Prompts

9. Keyword Research Strategy

Prompt: Create a keyword research strategy for [WEBSITE]. Include: seed keywords (20), long-tail opportunities (30), question-based keywords (15), commercial keywords (10), keyword clustering approach, and prioritization framework.

10. SEO Content Brief

Prompt: Create a detailed SEO content brief for [TARGET KEYWORD]. Include: search intent, target word count, title options, heading structure, entities/LSI keywords, internal linking targets, and SERP analysis insights.

Marketing Strategy Prompts

11. Marketing Funnel Designer

Prompt: Design a complete marketing funnel for [PRODUCT]. Map out: TOFU (awareness), MOFU (consideration), BOFU (decision), and retention. For each stage, define: channels, content types, CTAs, tools, and KPIs.

12. Influencer Outreach Strategy

Prompt: Create an influencer marketing strategy for [PRODUCT]. Define: target influencer profile, follower range tiers, outreach message templates, compensation structure, content guidelines, deliverables, and success metrics.
