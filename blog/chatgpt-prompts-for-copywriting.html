Landing Page Copy

Prompt:Write complete landing page copy for [PRODUCT/SERVICE]. Include: above-the-fold headline, subheadline, CTA, social proof section, benefits section (6 benefits), feature breakdown, FAQ (5 questions), guarantee section, and final CTA. Target audience: [DESCRIPTION].

Long-Form Sales Page

Prompt:Write a long-form sales page for [PRODUCT]. Use the PAS framework (Problem-Agitate-Solution). Include: pattern interrupt opening, story, problem identification, solution reveal, benefits, social proof, objection handling, offer stack, risk reversal, and urgency CTA. 2000+ words.

Cold Email Sequence

Prompt:Write a 5-email cold outreach sequence for [PRODUCT/SERVICE] targeting [ICP]. Include: subject lines (3 options each), personalized opener templates, value proposition, case study/social proof, soft CTA, follow-up timing, and breakup email. Keep each email under 150 words.

VSL (Video Sales Letter) Script

Prompt:Write a VSL script for [PRODUCT]. Length: 15-20 minutes. Include: hook, intro, problem identification, story, solution reveal, demonstration, benefits deep dive, social proof, objection handling, offer presentation, bonuses, guarantee, urgency, and CTA.

Ad Copy With 5 Formulas

Prompt:Write ad copy for [PRODUCT] using 5 different copywriting formulas: AIDA, PAS, BAB (Before-After-Bridge), FAB (Features-Advantages-Benefits), and the 4 Cs. For each formula, provide a complete ad suitable for [PLATFORM]. Explain why each works.

Email Subject Lines

Prompt:Generate 30 email subject lines for [EMAIL PURPOSE/TOPIC]. Use different psychological triggers: curiosity, urgency, scarcity, benefit, personalization, storytelling, controversy, social proof, question, and pattern interrupt. Group them by trigger type.

Objection Handling

Prompt:List the top 10 objections for [PRODUCT/SERVICE] and write a response for each. For each objection: restate it empathetically, provide a reframing, include evidence/proof, and pivot to a benefit. Format as a sales enablement document.
