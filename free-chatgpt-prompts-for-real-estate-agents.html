Free ChatGPT Prompts for Real Estate Agents

You juggle buyers, sellers, listings, and leads, and most of them expect a reply within the hour. The good agents sell houses; the great ones also write compelling listings, follow up without pestering, explain a counteroffer clearly, and keep a pipeline of people who might buy in six months warm. That is a lot of writing every single day. ChatGPT cannot show the house or negotiate the deal, but it can draft the words around it — a listing description that sells the lifestyle, a follow-up message after a showing, a newsletter blurb on the local market, or a gentle nudge to a lead gone quiet. The prompts below are tailored to real estate tasks and designed to sound like you wrote them, not like a robot. Fill in the property details or client situation, take the draft, and adjust the tone to match your brand. Pick the prompt that fits your day.

13 Free Prompts

Rental Property Analyzer

Analyze this rental property: Price [AMOUNT], Down payment [%], Interest rate [%], Rent [AMOUNT/month], Property tax [AMOUNT/year], Insurance [AMOUNT/year], HOA [AMOUNT/month]. Calculate: monthly mortgage payment, cash flow, cash-on-cash return, cap rate, 1% rule check, break-even occupancy, and 5-year projections. Recommend buy/pass with reasoning.

House Flipping Calculator

Analyze a house flip: Purchase price [AMOUNT], Repair costs [AMOUNT], After-Repair Value (ARV) [AMOUNT], Holding time [MONTHS], Financing [CASH/HARD MONEY/BANK]. Calculate: total project cost (purchase + repairs + holding + closing + selling), profit, ROI, 70% rule check, and best/worst case scenarios. Flag risks and include a deal-killing checklist.

Airbnb Listing Optimizer

Optimize my Airbnb listing for [PROPERTY TYPE] in [CITY]. Current title: [PASTE]. Rewrite: title (keyword-rich, benefit-driven), description (hooks guests, highlights unique features, addresses common concerns), amenities prioritization, photo order strategy, pricing recommendations, house rules (firm but welcoming), and automated message templates for guests.

Real Estate Market Analysis

Analyze the real estate market in [CITY/NEIGHBORHOOD] for [BUYING/SELLING/INVESTING]. Evaluate: median home prices and 5-year trend, days on market, inventory levels, price-to-rent ratio, job growth, population growth, school ratings, crime rates, development projects, and neighborhood trajectory. Recommend: buy, sell, or wait, with reasoning.

Tenant Screening Framework

Create a tenant screening framework for [PROPERTY TYPE] rental. Include: application requirements (income 3x rent, credit score minimum, references), background check process, red flags (not protected classes), approval/denial criteria documented consistently, adverse action process, security deposit logic, and lease addendum recommendations. Ensure Fair Housing compliance.

Lease Agreement Reviewer

Review this lease agreement for a tenant: [PASTE KEY TERMS]. Flag: unusual clauses, hidden costs, maintenance responsibility gaps, early termination penalties, rent increase terms, subletting restrictions, pet policies, security deposit terms, and ambiguous language. Provide negotiation points and specific counter-offer language for problematic clauses.

BRRRR Method Planner

Plan a BRRRR (Buy, Rehab, Rent, Refinance, Repeat) strategy for a property: Purchase [AMOUNT], Rehab [AMOUNT], ARV [AMOUNT], Expected rent [AMOUNT]. Calculate: total investment, refinance amount (75% of ARV), money left in deal, cash-on-cash return post-refinance, and how many repeats until target cash flow. Include risks: appraisal coming low, rehab overruns, refinance delays.

Property Management SOP

Create a property management standard operating procedure for [NUMBER] rental units. Cover: tenant onboarding, rent collection process, maintenance request handling (urgency tiers), vendor management, lease renewal process, move-out inspection, security deposit handling, eviction procedure, financial reporting, and emergency response protocol.

Wholesale Real Estate Deal Analyzer

Analyze a potential wholesale deal: Property [TYPE] at [ADDRESS/AREA], Estimated ARV [AMOUNT], Estimated repairs [AMOUNT], Seller's asking price [AMOUNT]. Calculate: maximum allowable offer (MAO) at 70% rule, assignment fee target, profit for end buyer, and whether this deal works. Include a script for negotiating with the seller and a buyer's list building strategy.

Mortgage Comparison Tool

Compare mortgage options for a [AMOUNT] loan: 30-year fixed at [%], 15-year fixed at [%], 7/1 ARM at [%], and [ANY OTHER]. For each: monthly payment, total interest paid, total cost, break-even analysis for paying points, and pros/cons. Factor in: how long I plan to stay ([YEARS]), my risk tolerance, and prepayment penalty considerations. Recommend with reasoning.

Rental Rate Optimizer

Help me set the optimal rent for my [PROPERTY TYPE] in [LOCATION]. Details: [BEDS/BATHS/SQFT/AMENITIES]. Analyze: comparable rentals (premium/average/below-average), seasonal pricing strategy, amenities premium, pet rent potential, utility responsibilities, and lease-incentive options (free month vs lower rate). Provide a pricing recommendation and adjustment triggers.

Real Estate Investment Portfolio Planner

I currently have [DESCRIBE PORTFOLIO - number of properties, types, equity, cash flow]. Goal: [TARGET - cash flow amount, portfolio size, retirement]. Create a 5-year acquisition plan: strategy (BRRRR, buy-and-hold, commercial), financing options, target markets, criteria checklist, annual milestones, tax considerations, and exit/transition strategy.

Home Inspection Checklist Generator

Create a comprehensive home inspection checklist for a [PROPERTY TYPE] buyer. Categories: exterior (roof, siding, drainage, landscaping), structural (foundation, walls, floors), systems (electrical, plumbing, HVAC), interior (windows, doors, appliances, fixtures), environmental (mold, radon, pests), and safety (smoke detectors, handrails, GFCI). Include red flags for each category.
