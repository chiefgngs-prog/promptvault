Free ChatGPT Prompts for YouTubers & Content Creators

You sit down to film and the script isn't there. The title you wrote last night looks weak in the morning light, the thumbnail idea feels generic, and your last video is underperforming with no clear reason why. Planning a content calendar, writing hooks that hold attention past the first ten seconds, and turning a rough idea into a structured outline eat the hours you'd rather spend actually creating. Then there are the descriptions, the timestamps, the pinned comment, the community post — every video ships with a pile of supporting text most creators dread. These 13 free ChatGPT prompts were built for the daily grind of running a channel. They help you draft titles worth testing, outline scripts beat by beat, write video descriptions that surface in search, and plan a month of content without starting from a blank page every time. No gimmicks, no promises of going viral — just reusable starting points you can paste in, edit, and make your own.

13 Free Prompts

Blog Post Outline Generator

Create a comprehensive blog post outline for [TOPIC]. Target keyword: [KEYWORD]. Include: compelling title options (5), meta description, H2/H3 structure, key points for each section, internal linking opportunities, and a strong CTA. Target word count: [NUMBER].

YouTube Script Writer

Write a YouTube video script for [TOPIC]. Length: [MINUTES] minutes. Include: hook (first 15 seconds), intro, main content with B-roll suggestions, pattern interrupts every 2 minutes, and a strong outro with CTA. Target audience: [DESCRIPTION].

Podcast Show Notes Generator

Create comprehensive podcast show notes for an episode about [TOPIC] with guest [NAME]. Include: episode summary, 5 key takeaways, timestamps for main topics, guest bio, resources/links mentioned, quotes, and a CTA. Make it SEO-friendly.

LinkedIn Article Writer

Write a LinkedIn article about [TOPIC] for [TARGET AUDIENCE]. Tone: professional yet conversational. Include: scroll-stopping hook, personal story element, 3-5 key insights with actionable advice, and an engagement-driving closing question. 800-1200 words.

Newsletter Issue Writer

Write a newsletter issue for [NEWSLETTER NAME] about [TOPIC]. Include: personal intro, main story/content, 3 practical tips, a 'worth reading' section (industry news), reader spotlight, and a CTA. Tone: [TONE]. Length: 600-800 words.

Repurpose Content Multiplier

Take this content: [PASTE CONTENT] and repurpose it into: 1) 5 Twitter/X threads, 2) 3 LinkedIn posts, 3) 1 blog post summary, 4) 5 Instagram captions, 5) 1 YouTube short script, 6) 1 email newsletter section. Adapt tone for each platform.

Content Repurposing Pipeline

Design a content repurposing system for [PRIMARY CONTENT TYPE]. Map how one piece of content transforms into: blog post, social media posts (5), email, video script, infographic outline, and podcast topic. Create a workflow diagram in text.

SEO Article Writer

Write an SEO-optimized article about [TOPIC]. Primary keyword: [KEYWORD]. Secondary keywords: [KEYWORDS]. Include: optimized H1, structured H2/H3 headings, meta description, FAQ section, internal linking suggestions, and natural keyword placement. 1500-2000 words.

Video Script Hook Generator

Generate 15 scroll-stopping video hooks for [TOPIC] on [PLATFORM]. Use different hook types: question, bold claim, story, statistic, contradiction, behind-the-scenes, and listicle. For each, explain why it works psychologically.

Infographic Content Planner

Plan an infographic about [TOPIC]. Include: main title, subtitle, data points (8-10) with sources, visual structure (sections and flow), color palette suggestion, icon recommendations, and a distribution strategy.

Instagram Carousel Designer

Plan a 10-slide Instagram carousel about [TOPIC]. For each slide, provide: slide number, headline, body text, visual suggestion, and design notes. Include a strong cover slide hook and CTA slide.

Case Study Writer

Write a compelling case study for [CLIENT/PROJECT]. Structure: Challenge, Solution, Implementation, Results (with metrics), Testimonial, and CTA. Include specific numbers and data points. Make it 800-1200 words, client-ready.

White Paper Outline

Create a detailed white paper outline for [TOPIC/INDUSTRY]. Include: executive summary, problem statement, market analysis, proposed solution, methodology, data/findings, recommendations, and conclusion. Suggest charts and visuals for each section.
