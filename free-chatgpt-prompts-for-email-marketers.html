Free ChatGPT Prompts for Email Marketers

The open rate lives or dies on the subject line, and most marketers are staring at the same twelve words they've already used. Writing welcome flows, abandoned-cart nudges, newsletters, win-back campaigns, and re-engagement sequences is a relentless cycle — each send needs a hook, a body that earns the click, and a CTA that doesn't sound like every other brand's. Burnout shows up as thin copy sent anyway. These 13 free ChatGPT prompts give you a faster path from blank screen to drafted send. Use them to generate subject-line variations you can A/B test, structure a multi-step welcome flow, write newsletter sections that stay on theme, and re-engage cold subscribers without resorting to gimmicky urgency. They produce solid first drafts grounded in your offer and audience, not generic filler. Edit them to match your brand voice and you're closer to send-ready than starting from scratch each time — you still set the strategy and approve every word before it goes out.

13 Free Prompts

Welcome Email Sequence

Write a 5-email welcome sequence for [BRAND/PRODUCT]. New subscriber just joined via [SOURCE]. Include: Email 1 (Welcome + deliver lead magnet), Email 2 (Brand story), Email 3 (Best content/resources), Email 4 (Social proof), Email 5 (Soft pitch). Subject lines for each.

Black Friday Email Strategy

Create a Black Friday email strategy for [STORE/BRAND]. Timeline: 2 weeks before through Cyber Monday. Include: teaser emails, early access, main sale, last chance, and extended sale. Subject lines, send times, segmentation, and discount strategy for each.

Re-engagement Campaign

Write a 4-email re-engagement sequence for inactive subscribers (no opens in 90 days) for [BRAND]. Include: 'We miss you' email, survey/feedback email, special offer email, and breakup email. Subject lines, timing, and segmentation strategy.

Product Launch Email Sequence

Write a 7-email product launch sequence for [PRODUCT]. Include: teaser (problem awareness), education (solution reveal), feature deep dive, social proof/testimonials, FAQ/objection handling, cart open with early bird, and last chance urgency. Subject lines for each.

Email Template Designer

Design 5 reusable email templates for [BRAND]: 1) Newsletter template, 2) Product announcement, 3) Promotional/sale, 4) Transactional (order confirmation), 5) Re-engagement. For each: provide layout description, copy template, image placement, and CTA positioning.

Email Segmentation Strategy

Create an email segmentation strategy for [BUSINESS]. Include: segmentation criteria (demographics, behavior, purchase history, engagement), segment profiles (8-10), content strategy per segment, automation triggers, and expected performance improvements.

Subject Line A/B Test Plan

Create an A/B testing plan for email subject lines for [BRAND]. Include: 10 test variations using different strategies (personalization, urgency, curiosity, numbers, questions, emojis), testing methodology, sample size calculation, success metrics, and learnings framework.

Holiday Email Calendar

Create a 6-month holiday email marketing calendar for [BRAND]. Include: all relevant holidays/events, email concepts for each, promotional strategy, send dates, segmentation, and content themes. Ensure alignment with [BRAND PRODUCTS/VALUES].

Cart Abandonment Flow

Write a 3-part cart abandonment email flow for [E-COMMERCE STORE]. Email 1 (1 hour): reminder. Email 2 (24 hours): social proof + assistance. Email 3 (72 hours): incentive/discount. Include subject lines, copy, CTAs, SMS integration, and success metrics.

Post-Purchase Email Flow

Design a post-purchase email flow for [PRODUCT]. Include: order confirmation, shipping notification, product usage tips, review request, cross-sell recommendation, loyalty program invite, and re-order reminder. Timing, subject lines, and copy for each.

Lead Nurture Educational Series

Write a 7-email educational nurture series for [COURSE/PRODUCT/SERVICE]. Topic: [SUBJECT]. Each email should teach one concept, build on the previous, and softly position [PRODUCT] as the solution. Include subject lines, content outline, and CTA for each.

Email Deliverability Audit

Create an email deliverability audit checklist for [SENDER DOMAIN]. Check: SPF, DKIM, DMARC records, sender reputation, spam triggers in content, list hygiene practices, engagement metrics, bounce handling, and warming strategy for new domains.

VIP Customer Email Strategy

Create a VIP email strategy for top customers of [BRAND]. Include: VIP criteria definition, exclusive content, early access strategy, personalized recommendations, birthday/anniversary emails, and VIP-only events/offers. Focus on retention and LTV.
