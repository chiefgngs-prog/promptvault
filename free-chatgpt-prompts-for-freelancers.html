13 Free Prompts

Strategy Offsite Facilitation

Design a 2-day strategy offsite for [COMPANY TYPE]'s leadership team of [NUMBER] people. Day 1: retrospection and market analysis. Day 2: strategy formulation and commitment. Include: detailed agenda, pre-read materials, discussion formats, decision-making frameworks, breakout exercises, output templates, and post-offsite follow-up plan.

Cost Transformation Strategy

Create a cost transformation strategy for [COMPANY TYPE] targeting [%] cost reduction. Analyze spend categories: people, technology, facilities, vendors, marketing, and operations. For each: identify reduction methods (eliminate, optimize, automate, outsource), estimate savings and implementation costs, assess impact on operations, and sequence initiatives by risk/reward.

Consulting Rate Calculator

Help me set consulting rates for [SERVICE/EXPERTISE]. My experience: [DESCRIBE]. Consider: target annual income, billable utilization rate (typically 60-70%), overhead costs, market rates by tier, value-based vs hourly pricing, retainer vs project pricing, and premium positioning factors. Calculate 3 rate scenarios and provide scripts for quoting rates to clients.

Thought Leadership Content Plan

Create a 90-day thought leadership content plan for a consultant in [FIELD]. Content types: LinkedIn posts (2x/week), long-form articles (biweekly), newsletter (weekly), speaking proposals (monthly), and one flagship resource (whitepaper/toolkit). For each: topics tied to [MY EXPERTISE], target audience, key message, CTA, and cross-posting strategy.

Client Retention Strategy

Create a client retention strategy for my consulting practice in [FIELD]. Current clients: [DESCRIBE]. Include: relationship nurturing cadence (check-ins between engagements), value-add content (insights, templates, benchmarks), expansion opportunities (scope growth, new service lines), feedback and improvement loop, renewal/re-engagement timeline, pricing for loyalty, and creating switching costs through deep expertise.

Landing Page Copywriter

Write complete landing page copy for [PRODUCT/SERVICE]. Include: above-the-fold headline, subheadline, CTA, social proof section, benefits section (6 benefits), feature breakdown, FAQ (5 questions), guarantee section, and final CTA. Target audience: [DESCRIPTION].

Sales Page Long-Form

Write a long-form sales page for [PRODUCT]. Use the PAS framework (Problem-Agitate-Solution). Include: pattern interrupt opening, story, problem identification, solution reveal, benefits, social proof, objection handling, offer stack, risk reversal, and urgency CTA. 2000+ words.

Cold Email Sequence

Write a 5-email cold outreach sequence for [PRODUCT/SERVICE] targeting [ICP]. Include: subject lines (3 options each), personalized opener templates, value proposition, case study/social proof, soft CTA, follow-up timing, and breakup email. Keep each email under 150 words.

VSL (Video Sales Letter) Script

Write a VSL script for [PRODUCT]. Length: 15-20 minutes. Include: hook, intro, problem identification, story, solution reveal, demonstration, benefits deep dive, social proof, objection handling, offer presentation, bonuses, guarantee, urgency, and CTA.

Cold Call Script Builder

Write a cold call script for [PRODUCT/SERVICE] targeting [ICP]. Include: opener (pattern interrupt), permission to speak, problem discovery questions, value proposition, qualifying questions, objection handling, and close/next step. Keep it conversational.

Discovery Call Framework

Create a discovery call framework for [PRODUCT/SERVICE]. Include: agenda structure, rapport building questions, situation questions, problem questions, implication questions, need-payoff questions, qualification criteria (BANT/MEDDIC), and next steps.

Sales Demo Script

Write a product demo script for [PRODUCT/SERVICE]. Include: agenda setting, discovery recap, tailored demo flow (focus on buyer's pain points), feature-to-benefit translation, interactive checkpoints, pricing presentation, and trial close.

Objection Handling Playbook

Create an objection handling playbook for [PRODUCT/SERVICE]. For each top 10 objections (price, timing, competitor, authority, need): provide empathetic acknowledgment, clarifying question, reframing response, and proof/social proof. Make it a quick-reference card.
