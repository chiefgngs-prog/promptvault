Angry Customer De-escalation

A customer is furious about [ISSUE]. Write a response using the HEARD framework: Hear, Empathize, Apologize, Resolve, Diagnose. Provide 3 response versions (email, live chat, phone script). Tone: calm, genuine, accountable. Never blame the customer. Include: immediate fix, goodwill gesture, and follow-up plan to rebuild trust.

Knowledge Base Article Writer

Write a knowledge base article for [COMMON CUSTOMER QUESTION]. Structure: clear title (phrased as a question), 1-sentence summary, step-by-step solution (numbered, with screenshots described), common error messages and fixes, video script suggestion, related articles, last updated date, and feedback prompt. Write at a 6th-grade reading level.

Define: priority levels (critical/high/medium/low), response time targets for each, resolution time targets, escalation matrix, business hours vs 24/7, breach consequences, reporting metrics, and continuous improvement process.

Customer Feedback Analysis

I have [NUMBER] customer feedback responses about [TOPIC]. Create an analysis framework: categorization scheme (bug, feature request, praise, complaint, question), sentiment scoring method, theme identification process, priority matrix (frequency x impact), reporting template, and a system for closing the loop with customers who provided feedback.

Self-Service Portal Planner

Include: content audit (what to migrate, create, retire), taxonomy/categories, search optimization strategy, article templates, video script library, community forum guidelines, chatbot integration plan, success metrics (deflection rate, CSAT), and content maintenance schedule.

Difficult Refund Decision

A customer is requesting a refund for [SITUATION] that falls [OUTSIDE/ON THE EDGE] of our policy. Our policy: [DESCRIBE]. Analyze: policy interpretation, customer lifetime value, word-of-mouth impact, precedent risk, and cost of the refund vs cost of the negative review. Provide a recommended decision and the exact response to the customer.

Support Metrics Dashboard

Design a support metrics dashboard for [TEAM]. Top-level: CSAT, NPS, First Response Time, Resolution Time, Reopen Rate, Ticket Volume, Backlog. For each metric: definition, target, current trend, breakdown options (by agent/channel/topic), alert thresholds, and weekly review cadence. Include coaching action triggers.

Goal: reduce inbound tickets and increase satisfaction.
