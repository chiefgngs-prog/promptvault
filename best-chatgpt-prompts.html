Why Most ChatGPT Prompts Fail

The difference between a bad prompt and a great prompt comes down to 5 elements:

  • Role assignment — Tell the AI who to be ("Act as a marketing strategist")
  • Context — Give it the background it needs
  • Task specification — Be specific about what you want
  • Output format — Tell it how to structure the response
  • Quality constraints — Set standards ("be specific," "avoid fluff")

Every prompt below uses all 5 elements. Just copy, paste, and replace the [BRACKETS].

Best ChatGPT Prompts for Marketing

1. Facebook Ad Copy Generator

Prompt: Write 10 variations of Facebook ad copy for [PRODUCT]. Target audience: [DESCRIPTION]. Include: 3 hook styles (question, bold statement, story), 2 body copy variations, clear CTAs, and suggested emoji usage. Optimize for high CTR.

This prompt generates ready-to-test ad variations. The key is specifying hook styles — it forces the AI to use different psychological angles rather than producing 10 variations that all sound the same.

2. Content Calendar Creator

Prompt: Create a 30-day content calendar for [BRAND] on [PLATFORM]. Include: daily post topics, content types (video, carousel, story, blog), captions, hashtag sets, best posting times, and content themes. Align with [MARKETING GOAL].

This gives you an entire month of content in one shot. No more staring at a blank screen wondering what to post.

3. Marketing Funnel Designer

Prompt: Design a complete marketing funnel for [PRODUCT/SERVICE]. Map out: TOFU (awareness), MOFU (consideration), BOFU (decision), and retention stages. For each stage, define: channels, content types, CTAs, tools needed, and KPIs to track.

Best ChatGPT Prompts for Copywriting

4. Landing Page Copywriter

Prompt: Write complete landing page copy for [PRODUCT/SERVICE]. Include: above-the-fold headline, subheadline, CTA, social proof section, benefits section (6 benefits), feature breakdown, FAQ (5 questions), guarantee section, and final CTA. Target audience: [DESCRIPTION].

This single prompt replaces a $500-2000 copywriter for your landing page. It produces a complete, structured page that you can then refine and polish.

5. Cold Email Sequence

Prompt: Write a 5-email cold outreach sequence for [PRODUCT/SERVICE] targeting [ICP]. Include: subject lines (3 options each), personalized opener templates, value proposition, case study/social proof, soft CTA, follow-up timing, and breakup email. Keep each email under 150 words.

Best ChatGPT Prompts for SEO

6. Keyword Research Strategy

Prompt: Create a keyword research strategy for [WEBSITE/INDUSTRY]. Include: seed keywords (20), long-tail opportunities (30), question-based keywords (15), commercial keywords (10), keyword clustering approach, search intent mapping, and prioritization framework.

7. SEO Content Brief Generator

Prompt: Create a detailed SEO content brief for [TARGET KEYWORD]. Include: search intent, target word count, title options, heading structure, entities/LSI keywords to include, internal linking targets, external linking suggestions, and SERP analysis insights.

Best ChatGPT Prompts for Business Strategy

8. SWOT Analysis

Prompt: Conduct a comprehensive SWOT analysis for [COMPANY/PRODUCT]. For each category (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats), provide 5 detailed points with explanations. Then suggest 3 strategic actions based on the analysis.

9. Pricing Strategy Optimizer

Prompt: Help me develop a pricing strategy for [PRODUCT/SERVICE]. Consider: production costs, competitor pricing, perceived value, target market willingness to pay, and market positioning. Provide 3 pricing tier options (basic, pro, enterprise) with justification for each.

Best ChatGPT Prompts for Sales

10. Objection Handling Playbook

Prompt: Create an objection handling playbook for [PRODUCT/SERVICE]. For each top 10 objections (price, timing, competitor, authority, need): provide empathetic acknowledgment, clarifying question, reframing response, and proof/social proof. Make it a quick-reference card.

How to Use These Prompts Effectively

  1. Replace ALL brackets — Every [BRACKET] needs to be replaced with your specific info. The more specific you are, the better the output.
  2. Iterate — If the first response isn't perfect, reply with "make it more [specific/casual/detailed]" to refine it.
  3. Save your favorites — When you find prompts that work for your business, save them in a document for easy access.
  4. Add constraints — Feel free to add "keep it under 500 words" or "use bullet points" to any prompt.
