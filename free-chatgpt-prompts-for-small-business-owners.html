13 Free Prompts

Exit Strategy Planner

Help me plan an exit strategy for [BUSINESS]. Compare options: acquisition, IPO, management buyout, merger, or liquidation. For each, analyze: timeline, valuation expectations, preparation needed, and probability of success. Recommend the best option with justification.

KPI Dashboard Designer

Design a KPI dashboard for [BUSINESS TYPE/DEPARTMENT]. Identify the top 10 KPIs to track, categorized by: financial, customer, operational, and growth metrics. For each KPI, define: what it measures, target value, frequency of measurement, and data source.

Lean Startup Experiment Designer

Design a lean startup experiment to validate [HYPOTHESIS]. Define: the hypothesis, minimum viable test, success criteria, metrics to track, timeline, budget, and what we'd do based on pass/fail results.

Business Plan Executive Summary

Write a compelling 2-page executive summary for a business plan about [BUSINESS IDEA]. Cover: problem, solution, market opportunity, business model, competitive advantage, team, financial highlights, and funding requirements. Make it investor-ready.

Merger & Acquisition Target Screener

Define criteria for identifying acquisition targets for [MY COMPANY] looking to [EXPAND INTO MARKET/ADD CAPABILITY]. Create a scoring framework evaluating: strategic fit, financial health, cultural compatibility, technology assets, and valuation reasonableness.

Change Management Plan

Create a change management plan for [ORGANIZATIONAL CHANGE]. Include: stakeholder analysis, communication strategy, training plan, resistance management approach, success metrics, and a 90-day rollout timeline.

Vendor Evaluation Matrix

Create a vendor evaluation framework for [CATEGORY OF SERVICE/PRODUCT]. Define 10 evaluation criteria with weights, a scoring system (1-5), and questions to ask each vendor. Provide a template comparison table for evaluating 3 vendors side by side.

Business Continuity Plan

Develop a business continuity plan for [BUSINESS TYPE]. Address: critical business functions, potential disruptions (natural, cyber, pandemic, supply chain), recovery strategies, communication protocols, and minimum viable operations for each scenario.

Franchise Model Designer

Design a franchise model for [BUSINESS]. Define: franchise fee structure, royalty rates, territory rights, training program, operational manuals needed, franchisee requirements, and support systems. Include financial projections for both franchisor and franchisee.

Startup Valuation Calculator

Help me value my startup: [DESCRIPTION]. Stage: [PRE-SEED/SEED/SERIES A]. Revenue: [AMOUNT]. Growth rate: [X%]. Use multiple methods: Berkus, Scorecard, VC Method, Comparable Analysis, and DCF. Explain each result and recommend a fair valuation range.

Negotiation Strategy Planner

Prepare a negotiation strategy for [NEGOTIATION SCENARIO]. Analyze: my BATNA, their likely BATNA, key interests (not positions), ZOPA estimation, concession plan, walk-away point, and tactics for creating value. Role-play potential objections.

Annual Operating Plan Builder

Create an annual operating plan for [BUSINESS]. Include: strategic objectives, departmental goals, revenue targets, budget allocation, hiring plan, key projects, quarterly milestones, and risk factors. Format as a clear executive document.

Board Meeting Prep

Prepare materials for a board meeting for [COMPANY]. Create: CEO update summary, financial performance dashboard, key wins, challenges and mitigation plans, strategic decisions needing board input, and proposed resolutions. Keep it concise and data-driven.
