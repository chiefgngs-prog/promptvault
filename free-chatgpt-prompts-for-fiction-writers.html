Free ChatGPT Prompts for Fiction Writers

You know the feeling: a character who will not behave, a plot that stalls in act two, dialogue that reads like two robots exchanging information, or a blank page on a night you finally had time to write. The story lives in your head, but getting it onto the page is the whole fight. ChatGPT cannot write your novel for you, and you would not want it to, but it is a sharp brainstorming partner for the parts that stall. It can generate twenty character names in the right cultural register, workshop a scene until the dialogue sounds human, pressure-test your plot for holes, suggest what happens next when you are stuck, or build a setting bible from your scattered notes. The prompts below are built to spark and refine your own work, not to replace your voice. Take what is useful, ignore what is not, and keep writing the parts only you can write. Pick a prompt and get unstuck.

13 Free Prompts

Story Premise Generator

Generate 10 unique story premises for [GENRE]. Each premise should include: a compelling hook, the protagonist and their flaw, the central conflict, the stakes, what makes it fresh/different from existing works, and a one-sentence logline. Make them range from grounded to high-concept.

Character Development Deep Dive

Help me develop a complex character for my [GENRE] story. Starting with: [BASIC DESCRIPTION]. Create: full backstory (childhood wound, defining moment), personality (MBTI, strengths, fatal flaw), physical description, speech pattern, relationships, secrets, fears, desires, arc (what they need vs what they want), and their voice in first person.

World Building Guide

Help me build the world for my [GENRE] story. The core concept: [DESCRIBE]. Develop: geography and climate, political system, economy and currency, religion/beliefs, social hierarchy, technology/magic system (with rules and costs), history (3 major events), daily life details, cultural norms, and potential conflicts within the world.

Plot Structure Architect

Architect a [LENGTH] plot for my [GENRE] story about [CONCEPT]. Use the [Save the Cat / Hero's Journey / Three-Act] structure. For each beat: describe what happens, how it raises stakes, character development moment, estimated page/word count, and the emotional arc. Include subplot integration points and a twist suggestion.

Dialogue Enhancement Coach

Improve this dialogue to make it natural and character-specific: [PASTE DIALOGUE]. Rewrite it to: give each character a distinct voice, add subtext (what's unsaid), remove exposition-heavy lines, add action beats, vary rhythm (short punches and longer flows), and advance plot or reveal character. Explain what changed and why.

Scene Structure Builder

Help me structure a critical scene: [DESCRIBE THE SCENE]. Define: the scene goal (what the POV character wants), conflict (what stands in their way), disaster/complication, sequel (reaction, dilemma, decision), emotional shift from start to end, sensory details to include, and how it connects to the next scene. Target: [WORD COUNT] words.

Poem Generator

Write a [STYLE - sonnet/haiku/free verse/spoken word] poem about [TOPIC/THEME]. Incorporate: vivid imagery (show don't tell), metaphor and simile, rhythm that matches the mood, line breaks for emphasis, and emotional progression. If formal style, follow the rules strictly. Provide a brief note on craft choices.

Short Story Completer

Here's the beginning of my short story: [PASTE]. Continue it for [WORD COUNT] words. Maintain: my writing style and voice, established character dynamics, story tone, pacing, and planted hints. Escalate the conflict toward a turning point. Do not resolve the story yet—end on a hook that compels reading.

Song Lyric Writer

Write lyrics for a [GENRE] song about [THEME/EMOTION]. Structure: Verse 1 (set the scene), Pre-chorus (build tension), Chorus (emotional core/hook), Verse 2 (develop the story), Pre-chorus, Chorus, Bridge (shift perspective/revelation), Chorus, Outro. Include rhyme scheme, suggested melody direction, and rhythm notes. Make it singable.

Screenplay Formatter

Convert this scene description into properly formatted screenplay format: [PASTE]. Format with: scene heading (INT./EXT., LOCATION, TIME), action lines (present tense, visual), character names (centered, caps), dialogue, parentheticals (sparingly), and transitions. Follow industry-standard formatting. Improve description to be cinematic.

Editing Pass Guide

Act as a developmental editor. Review this story: [PASTE]. Provide feedback on: pacing (where it drags or rushes), character consistency, plot holes, showing vs telling, dialogue naturalness, sensory engagement, emotional resonance, opening hook strength, and ending satisfaction. Prioritize: 3 things that work, 3 critical fixes, 3 polish suggestions.

Backstory Generator

Create a compelling backstory for [CHARACTER NAME/TYPE] in a [GENRE] setting. Include: birth/childhood circumstances, one traumatic event that shaped them, one joyous memory they cling to, relationship with parents, defining choice they made, skill they developed as coping, a secret they've never told, and how all of this explains their current behavior.

Villain Creator

Create a complex, memorable villain for my [GENRE] story. The hero is [DESCRIBE HERO]. The villain should: have understandable motivations (not evil for evil's sake), be the hero's mirror or shadow, have a philosophy that's almost convincing, possess strengths the hero lacks, have a personal connection to the hero, and a vulnerability the hero can exploit.
