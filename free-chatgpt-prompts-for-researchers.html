Free ChatGPT Prompts for Researchers

You read more papers than anyone should, synthesize sprawling literatures into something coherent, frame a research question worth chasing, and then write it all up in a voice that survives peer review. The intellectual work is hard enough; the mechanical work — summarizing, formatting, drafting, editing — is what eats your evenings. ChatGPT is useful here as a reading and writing assistant, with one critical caveat: it fabricates citations and will invent sources that look real, so it must never be your source of truth for facts or references. Used correctly, it can summarize a paper you paste in, suggest gaps across a body of literature, turn your findings notes into a draft abstract, rephrase a dense paragraph into clear prose, or generate search terms for a database. These prompts are built to support your analysis with verified material you provide, never to generate claims or citations. Feed it your text, verify everything, and write. Grab a prompt below.

13 Free Prompts

Literature Review Framework

Help me structure a literature review on [TOPIC] for a [GRANT/THESIS/PAPER]. Define: search strategy (databases, keywords, inclusion/exclusion criteria), thematic organization approach, synthesis matrix template (author, year, methodology, findings, limitations), gap identification method, and narrative structure. Provide the first 3 paragraphs as a template.

Research Question Refiner

My broad research interest is [TOPIC]. Help me refine it into a research question using the FINER criteria (Feasible, Interesting, Novel, Ethical, Relevant). Generate 5 specific research questions, evaluate each against the criteria, identify the strongest one, and convert it into a testable hypothesis with independent and dependent variables clearly defined.

Methodology Selector

My research question is: [PASTE]. Help me choose the best methodology. Compare: quantitative vs qualitative vs mixed methods. For the recommended approach: justify the choice, identify the specific design (experiment, survey, case study, ethnography, etc.), define sampling strategy, outline data collection tools, and address validity/reliability concerns.

Survey Design Expert

Design a survey to measure [CONSTRUCT/VARIABLE] for [POPULATION]. Create 20 questions mixing types (Likert, multiple choice, open-ended, ranking). Ensure: face validity, construct validity, avoidance of leading/double-barreled questions, logical flow, demographic section placement, attention checks, and estimated completion time. Include a pre-test plan.

Grant Proposal Writer

Write a grant proposal for [FUNDING BODY] on [RESEARCH TOPIC]. Structure: specific aims (2-3 clear objectives), significance (gap in knowledge and impact), innovation (what's new), approach (preliminary data, methodology, timeline), budget justification, and broader impacts. Make it compelling to reviewers who may not be experts in my specific sub-field.

Systematic Review Protocol

Create a systematic review protocol following PRISMA guidelines for the question: [RESEARCH QUESTION]. Define: eligibility criteria (PICOS), information sources, search strategy (with example Boolean string), selection process, data extraction template, risk of bias assessment tool, synthesis method, and heterogeneity handling plan.

Academic Paper Abstract Writer

Write a structured abstract for my paper about [TOPIC]. Key findings: [SUMMARIZE]. Format: Background (2 sentences on context and gap), Methods (3 sentences on approach), Results (3 sentences on key findings with key numbers), Conclusions (2 sentences on implications), and Keywords (6-8 terms). Target: 250 words. Make every sentence information-dense.

Thesis Structure Planner

Create a detailed thesis/dissertation outline for [TOPIC] at [MASTERS/PHD] level. For each chapter: purpose, estimated page count, section breakdown, key arguments/claims, literature to cite, methodology details, and how it connects to the next chapter. Include milestone timeline: proposal, data collection, draft, defense. Flag common committee concerns per chapter.

Data Collection Plan

Design a data collection plan for my study on [TOPIC]. Method: [SURVEY/INTERVIEW/OBSERVATION/EXPERIMENT]. Include: operationalization of variables, instrument design (or selection of validated instruments), sampling plan (type, size calculation, recruitment strategy), data management procedures, quality control measures, ethics/IRB considerations, and timeline.

Peer Review Response Letter

I received peer reviews for my paper on [TOPIC]. Reviewer comments: [PASTE]. Draft a response letter. For each comment: quote the reviewer, respond respectfully, describe the exact change made (with page/line numbers), or provide a reasoned rebuttal. Start with a summary of major changes. Tone: appreciative, professional, non-defensive. Include revised manuscript notes.

Research Ethics Application

Prepare an IRB/ethics application for my study: [DESCRIBE STUDY]. Complete: study purpose, research design summary, participant population and recruitment, informed consent process, data privacy and storage plan, risk assessment (physical, psychological, social, economic), risk minimization strategies, debriefing plan, and researcher qualifications.

Conference Presentation Builder

Create a conference presentation for [CONFERENCE NAME/TYPE] based on my research: [DESCRIBE FINDINGS]. Duration: [MINUTES]. Outline: slide-by-slide breakdown, each with title, key points (max 4 bullets), visual description (chart, diagram, image), speaker notes, and audience engagement moments. Start with a hook, build the narrative, end with impact and Q&A prep.

Meta-Analysis Plan

Design a meta-analysis plan for [RESEARCH QUESTION]. Define: effect size metric (Cohen's d, odds ratio, correlation), literature search strategy, inclusion/exclusion criteria, coding sheet template, heterogeneity assessment (I², Q-test), publication bias detection (funnel plot, Egger's test), subgroup analysis plan, and software choice (RevMan, Comprehensive Meta-Analysis, R).
