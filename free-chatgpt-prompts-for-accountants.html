Free ChatGPT Prompts for Accountants

The numbers are the easy part. What eats an accountant's day is translating them — explaining a deduction to a confused client, writing a management report that non-finance owners will actually read, drafting a polite-but-firm email about an overdue invoice, and turning dense tax changes into a short update that doesn't bury the point. Season-end turns this into a volume problem: dozens of clients, each needing clear, accurate, jargon-free communication, all at once. These 13 free ChatGPT prompts handle the writing that surrounds the figures. Use them to draft client-facing explanations of complex tax issues, structure advisory notes and board reports, write onboarding and reminder emails, and summarize new regulation into plain language. They give you a clean first draft to review and adjust — accuracy still rests with you, but the blank-page drafting time drops sharply. Edit the specifics, keep your firm's tone, and send — paste any prompt in, drop in the client's details, and you've moved past the hardest sentence.

13 Free Prompts

Net Worth Tracker

Create a system to track and grow my net worth. Current assets: [LIST]. Current liabilities: [LIST]. Design: a balance sheet template, monthly update process, growth strategies for each asset category, debt reduction priorities, milestone celebrations, and an annual net worth statement format. Include tracking of liquid vs illiquid assets.

Insurance Coverage Audit

Audit my insurance coverage. Current policies: [LIST]. Analyze: coverage gaps, over-insurance areas, deductible optimization, policy bundle opportunities, term life vs whole life analysis, disability insurance need calculation, umbrella policy recommendation, and annual review checklist. Estimate potential savings from optimization.

Passive Income Blueprint

Create a passive income blueprint for my situation. Starting capital: [AMOUNT]. Skills: [LIST]. Risk tolerance: [LEVEL]. Evaluate 10 passive income streams: dividend investing, REITs, peer-to-peer lending, digital products, course creation, affiliate marketing, rental properties, royalties, high-yield savings, and index fund portfolio. Rank by: capital needed, time to income, and risk.

Negotiation Script: Bills

Create scripts for negotiating lower rates on: internet/cable, phone bill, car insurance, gym membership, and subscription services. For each: research to do beforehand, exact opening line, negotiation tactics, what to ask for, escalation path (ask for retention department), and backup providers. Target: reduce monthly expenses by 15-20%.

Estate Planning Checklist

Create an estate planning checklist for my situation: [SINGLE/MARRIED/PARENT/HOMEOWNER]. Cover: will (what to include), trust (do I need one?), power of attorney, healthcare directive, beneficiary designations on all accounts, guardianship (if children), digital asset plan, letter of intent, document storage, and review schedule. Estimate cost for each.

Credit Score Improvement Plan

My current credit score is [SCORE]. Target: [SCORE]. Analyze: the 5 factors (payment history, utilization, age, mix, inquiries) and identify my weak spots. Create a 90-day improvement plan with: specific actions for each factor, dispute strategy for errors, credit utilization optimization, authorized user strategy, and monitoring system.

Charitable Giving Strategy

Help me create a charitable giving plan. Annual giving budget: [AMOUNT]. Causes I care about: [LIST]. Create: giving criteria for evaluating charities, research process (Charity Navigator, GiveWell), portfolio approach (local/global/impact), tax-efficient giving (bunching, DAF, appreciated stock), and an annual giving review process.

Tax-Loss Harvesting Strategy

Explain and create a tax-loss harvesting strategy for my investment portfolio: [DESCRIBE HOLDINGS]. Rules to follow: wash-sale rule (30 days), substantial similarity test, replacing with similar-but-not-identical assets, maintaining asset allocation, $3,000 ordinary income offset limit, carryforward rules. Create a step-by-step process with specific replacement suggestions for my holdings.

Money Conversation Guide for Couples

Create a guide for couples to discuss money openly. Cover: setting up the conversation (timing, environment, ground rules), key topics to align on (spending priorities, debt philosophy, savings goals, financial independence, combining vs separating finances), navigating income disparity, handling financial disagreements, money personality assessment, and creating a shared financial vision. Include conversation prompts.

KPI Tree Builder

Build a KPI tree for [BUSINESS TYPE]. Root: [NORTH STAR METRIC]. Decompose into: acquisition metrics, activation metrics, retention metrics, revenue metrics, and referral metrics. For each leaf metric: definition, formula, data source, target, frequency, owner, and how improving it affects the parent metric. Show the math connecting each level.

Survey Data Analyzer

I collected survey data about [TOPIC] with [NUMBER] responses. Questions: [LIST]. Create an analysis plan: response rate assessment, demographic breakdown, Likert scale analysis (distribution + central tendency), open-ended coding framework, cross-tabulation plan, significance testing, key insights template, and visualization recommendations for reporting.

Anomaly Detection Guide

Design an anomaly detection system for [DATA TYPE - metrics, logs, transactions]. Specify: what constitutes an anomaly for this use case, detection methods (statistical, ML, rule-based), threshold setting approach, false positive management, alerting design, root cause analysis workflow, and feedback loop for improving detection accuracy over time.

Pricing Analytics Framework

Create a pricing analytics framework for [PRODUCT/SERVICE]. Analyze: price elasticity estimation method, competitor price tracking, segment-based pricing opportunities, bundling analysis, promotional effectiveness, willingness-to-pay research plan, price testing strategy, and margin impact modeling. Provide a dashboard layout for pricing decisions.
