Free ChatGPT Prompts for HR and Recruiters

You write job descriptions that attract the right people and not the wrong ones, screen a flood of applications, run interviews that actually reveal fit, and deliver bad news without burning bridges. Half the role is communication, and half of that is repetitive — the offer letters, the rejection emails, the policy explanations, the onboarding steps nobody remembers to document. ChatGPT speeds up exactly this kind of writing. It can draft a job posting from a few bullet points, generate role-specific interview questions, build a structured interview rubric, or turn your rough notes into a clean onboarding plan. Use care with anything touching personal data or legal weight — these prompts are meant for drafting language from your input, not for storing candidate information or replacing a lawyer on policy questions. Keep sensitive details out, edit the output, and route anything legally binding through review. Grab the prompt you need below.

13 Free Prompts

Job Description Writer

Write a compelling job description for [JOB TITLE] at [COMPANY TYPE]. Include: company overview (2 sentences, exciting), role summary, 5 key responsibilities (outcome-focused not task-list), requirements (must-have vs nice-to-have), what we offer, growth opportunities, and inclusive language. Make it sell the role. Avoid jargon and generic phrases.

Interview Rubric Designer

Create a structured interview rubric for [JOB TITLE]. Define 5 competencies. For each: write 1 behavioral question, 1 situational question, what excellent/good/poor answers look like (with examples), follow-up probes, red flags, and scoring guide (1-5 scale). Include space for notes and an overall recommendation matrix.

Onboarding Plan Builder

Design a 90-day onboarding plan for a new [JOB TITLE]. Week 1: orientation and setup. Weeks 2-4: learning and shadowing. Weeks 5-8: guided contribution. Weeks 9-12: independent work. For each phase: goals, activities, training resources, check-ins, deliverables, success metrics, and feedback touchpoints. Include pre-day-one preparation.

Performance Review Template

Create a 360-degree performance review template for [ROLE]. Sections: self-assessment, peer feedback (3 questions), manager assessment, goal review, competency evaluation (rate 1-5 with evidence), development areas, career aspirations, and next-period goal-setting. Include calibration guide for managers and example language for each rating.

Employee Engagement Survey

Design an employee engagement survey for [COMPANY SIZE] company. Create 25 questions across: job satisfaction, manager relationship, team dynamics, growth opportunities, company culture, compensation fairness, work-life balance, and intent to stay. Mix Likert scale and open-ended. Include benchmark targets and an action-planning framework for results.

Compensation Strategy Builder

Design a compensation strategy for [COMPANY TYPE/SIZE]. Include: pay philosophy (market leader, match, or lag), salary band structure (how many bands, width, overlap), variable pay design (bonus/commission/equity), benefits package tiers, pay equity monitoring process, geographic adjustment policy, and communication guidelines for transparency.

Employee Handbook Outline

Create an employee handbook outline for a [STARTUP/SMALL BUSINESS/MID-SIZE] company. Cover: company mission and values, employment policies, code of conduct, compensation and benefits, leave policies, remote work policy, anti-harassment, health and safety, performance management, grievance procedure, and acknowledgments. Tone: warm but legally sound.

Conflict Resolution Protocol

Create a workplace conflict resolution protocol for [COMPANY TYPE]. Define: levels of conflict, when to self-resolve vs involve HR, mediation process steps, documentation requirements, confidentiality guidelines, escalation matrix, timeline expectations, and follow-up process. Include scripts for managers having initial conversations with conflicting parties.

Training Program Designer

Design a training program for [SKILL/TOPIC] for [AUDIENCE - new hires, managers, all staff]. Include: learning objectives, prerequisite knowledge, module breakdown (each with content, activity, assessment), delivery format (live/async/hybrid), materials needed, duration, measurement of learning transfer, and reinforcement plan.

Remote Work Policy Writer

Write a comprehensive remote/hybrid work policy for [COMPANY TYPE]. Address: eligibility criteria, equipment and stipend, core hours and flexibility, communication expectations, meeting norms, performance measurement (outcomes not hours), security requirements, tax/legal considerations, wellbeing support, and team-building practices.

Termination Script & Checklist

I need to terminate an employee for [REASON]. Create: a pre-termination checklist (documentation, legal review, final pay), script for the conversation (respectful, clear, brief), logistics (access revocation, equipment return, transition plan), communication to the team, and post-termination follow-up. Include DOs and DON'Ts to minimize legal risk.

Diversity & Inclusion Action Plan

Create a D&I action plan for [COMPANY TYPE/SIZE]. Include: current state assessment, goals (hiring, retention, promotion, culture), sourcing strategies for diverse candidates, interview process bias mitigation, employee resource group framework, inclusive policies checklist, training plan, measurement metrics, and accountability structure.

Exit Interview Guide

Design an exit interview process for departing [ROLE TYPE]. Create: interview invitation message, 15 questions covering reasons for leaving, job satisfaction, manager relationship, what would have made them stay, feedback on company culture, suggestions for improvement, and whether they'd recommend the company. Include analysis framework for trends.
