Free ChatGPT Prompts for Fitness Coaches

You write programs for a dozen different bodies and goals, keep clients accountable when motivation drops, answer the same nutrition questions over and over, and still have to post on social media to bring in new clients. The coaching happens in person or on the call, but the writing around it fills the rest of your day. ChatGPT cannot assess movement or know your client's history, but it can take a load off the admin side. It can draft a workout template from your parameters, write a check-in message that actually gets a reply, turn your expertise into a quick nutrition explainer, or outline a week of social posts that sound like you. These prompts are built to support your professional judgment, not replace it — you set the goals, contraindications, and tone, and the model fills in the structure. Add your specifics, review for safety, and send. Pick your prompt below.

13 Free Prompts

Personalized Workout Plan Builder

Create a [NUMBER]-week workout plan for a [AGE]-year-old [MALE/FEMALE], [FITNESS LEVEL], goal: [LOSE WEIGHT/BUILD MUSCLE/ENDURANCE/GENERAL FITNESS]. Equipment: [GYM/HOME/BODYWEIGHT]. Available: [DAYS] days/week, [MINUTES] per session. Include: weekly split, exercises with sets/reps/rest, progressive overload plan, warm-up/cool-down, and deload week. Explain the rationale.

Meal Plan Generator

Create a 7-day meal plan for [GOAL - weight loss/muscle gain/maintenance]. Calories: [TARGET]. Dietary restrictions: [LIST]. Preferences: [LIST]. Include: 3 meals + 2 snacks daily, macro breakdown, grocery shopping list organized by store section, prep instructions for batch cooking, and estimated daily cost. Make it realistic and sustainable.

Habit-Based Nutrition Coach

Act as a nutrition coach. I'm [CURRENT EATING HABITS], [GOAL], [RESTRICTIONS]. Don't give me a restrictive diet. Instead, create 10 progressive weekly habits (e.g., week 1: drink water before each meal). For each habit: why it works, how to implement, common obstacles, success criteria, and how it builds on the previous week. Focus on sustainability over speed.

Sleep Optimization Protocol

Create a sleep optimization protocol. Current sleep issues: [DESCRIBE - trouble falling asleep, waking up, insufficient hours]. Address: sleep environment (temperature, light, noise, mattress), pre-sleep routine (90 min wind-down), daytime habits (caffeine, exercise, light exposure), sleep schedule consistency, relaxation techniques, and when to seek medical evaluation.

Stress Management Toolkit

Create a personalized stress management toolkit for someone dealing with [TYPE OF STRESS - work, personal, financial, health]. Include: 3-minute emergency techniques (box breathing, grounding), daily practices (10 min), weekly reset activities, monthly self-assessment, burnout warning signs, boundary-setting scripts, and professional help criteria.

Running Program Designer

Create a [NUMBER]-week running program for a [BEGINNER/INTERMEDIATE/ADVANCED] runner training for [5K/10K/HALF/MARATHON]. Current ability: [DESCRIBE]. Include: weekly schedule (runs with type - easy/tempo/long/interval), cross-training, strength exercises for runners, progression plan, rest/recovery protocol, and race-day strategy. Include injury prevention tips.

Calorie & Macro Calculator

Calculate my calorie and macro targets: [AGE], [GENDER], [HEIGHT], [WEIGHT], [ACTIVITY LEVEL], goal: [LOSE/GAIN/MAINTAIN]. Calculate: BMR (Mifflin-St Jeor), TDEE, target calories, protein/fat/carb split with reasoning, adjustment protocol (what to change if not progressing after 2 weeks), refeed day strategy, and tracking recommendations.

Home Gym Setup Planner

Design a home gym for [BUDGET] and [SPACE - room/garage/corner]. Fitness goals: [DESCRIBE]. Recommend: essential equipment ranked by priority, cost breakdown for 3 tiers (budget/quality/premium), space layout description, flooring and ventilation needs, storage solutions, and alternatives for each major exercise. Calculate cost-per-use over 5 years vs gym membership.

Injury Prevention & Recovery

I do [ACTIVITY/SPORT] and keep getting [TYPE OF INJURY]. Create an injury prevention and recovery plan: pre-activity warm-up (dynamic), mobility exercises targeting my weak points, strength imbalances to correct, post-activity recovery routine, warning signs to watch for, return-to-activity protocol after injury, and when to see a physical therapist.

Healthy Meal Prep System

Design a meal prep system for [NUMBER] people, [DIETARY NEEDS], [DAYS] of meals per prep session. Include: master ingredient list, batch-cook recipes (3 proteins, 3 carbs, 3 vegetables), mix-and-match combination guide, storage containers and organization, reheating instructions, food safety guidelines, and a 2-hour Sunday prep schedule.

Mental Health Journaling Guide

Create a mental health journaling system for managing [ANXIETY/DEPRESSION/STRESS/OVERWHELM]. Include: morning check-in prompts (mood, intention, gratitude), evening reflection (wins, challenges, tomorrow's focus), CBT thought records (situation, thought, evidence, reframe), mood tracking system, triggers log, and weekly pattern review. Include privacy and safety notes.

Desk Worker Health Protocol

Create a health protocol for someone who sits [HOURS] hours daily at a desk. Include: ergonomic setup checklist, movement breaks (every 30/60/90 min), desk stretches (neck, shoulders, hips, wrists), posture correction exercises, eye strain prevention (20-20-20), hydration system, nutrition strategies for energy, and after-work mobility routine.

Water Intake Optimizer

Create a personalized hydration plan. Current intake: [AMOUNT]. Activity level: [DESCRIBE]. Climate: [DESCRIBE]. Calculate: optimal daily intake, schedule (when to drink throughout the day), electrolyte considerations, signs of over/under-hydration, tracking system, hydration-rich foods to add, and adjustment for exercise, heat, and altitude.
