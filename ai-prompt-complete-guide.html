The 5-Element Prompt Framework

Every prompt in this guide uses this structure:

  1. Role Assignment - Tell the AI who to be
  2. Context - Background information
  3. Task - What you want done
  4. Output Format - How to structure the response
  5. Constraints - Quality requirements

Category 1: Business Strategy (25 Prompts)

1. Business Model Canvas Generator

Act as a business strategist. Create a complete Business Model Canvas for [BUSINESS IDEA]. Include: 1) Key Partners, 2) Key Activities, 3) Key Resources, 4) Value Propositions, 5) Customer Relationships, 6) Channels, 7) Customer Segments, 8) Cost Structure, 9) Revenue Streams. Be specific and actionable for each section.

2. SWOT Analysis Deep Dive

Conduct a comprehensive SWOT analysis for [COMPANY/PRODUCT]. For each category (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats), provide 5 detailed points with explanations. Then suggest 3 strategic actions based on the analysis.

3. Competitor Analysis Framework

Act as a competitive intelligence analyst. Analyze [MY COMPANY] vs [TOP 3 COMPETITORS]. Compare across: pricing, features, target market, marketing strategy, strengths, weaknesses, and market positioning. Output as a detailed comparison table with strategic recommendations.

4. Pricing Strategy Optimizer

Help me develop a pricing strategy for [PRODUCT/SERVICE]. Consider: production costs, competitor pricing, perceived value, target market willingness to pay, and market positioning. Provide 3 pricing tier options (basic, pro, enterprise) with justification for each.

5. Investor Pitch Deck Outline

Create a compelling 12-slide investor pitch deck outline for [STARTUP]. Include: Problem, Solution, Market Size, Product, Traction, Business Model, Go-to-Market Strategy, Competition, Team, Financial Projections, The Ask, and Appendix. For each slide, provide key talking points and suggested visuals.
