Free ChatGPT Prompts for Social Media Managers

Managing multiple accounts means writing all day — and still running out of ideas by Wednesday. Every platform wants its own tone, the calendar needs three weeks of content by Friday, and the client just asked why engagement dipped on a post you barely had time to write. Captions, hooks, reply templates, hashtag sets, campaign concepts, month-end reports: the work piles up faster than any single brain can draft it well. These 13 free ChatGPT prompts take the repetitive writing off your plate. They help you generate caption variations tuned to each platform, build out a week of content from one campaign idea, draft on-brand replies to common comments, and turn raw analytics into a summary a client can actually read. They won't replace your judgment or your brand voice — they clear the blank-page friction so you can get to the good part faster. Each prompt is a starting point you adjust to the brand — paste it in, swap the specifics, and you've got a draft worth scheduling instead of another empty text box.

13 Free Prompts

Instagram Growth Strategy

Create a 90-day Instagram growth strategy for [BRAND/ACCOUNT]. Current followers: [NUMBER]. Goal: [NUMBER]. Include: content pillars (5), posting frequency, Reels strategy, hashtag strategy, engagement routine, collaboration plan, and milestone tracking.

Instagram Caption Generator

Write 10 Instagram caption variations for this post: [DESCRIBE IMAGE/VIDEO]. Include different styles: storytelling, educational, inspirational, controversial, listicle, question, behind-the-scenes, and minimal. Include relevant hashtags (20-30) and CTAs.

LinkedIn Growth Strategy

Create a LinkedIn growth strategy for [PROFESSIONAL/BRAND]. Goal: [GROWTH TARGET] in 90 days. Include: profile optimization checklist, content pillars, posting frequency, engagement strategy, connection strategy, DM templates, and analytics tracking.

Twitter/X Growth Playbook

Create a Twitter/X growth playbook for [NICHE/PERSONA]. Include: profile optimization, content types (threads, hot takes, insights, stories), posting frequency, engagement strategy, reply guy method, collaboration tactics, and viral framework.

Social Media Crisis Management

Create a social media crisis management plan for [BRAND]. Include: crisis definition levels (1-5), response team roles, response time targets, approval process, message templates for common scenarios, monitoring tools, and post-crisis recovery steps.

Content Repurposing Matrix

Create a content repurposing matrix for [PRIMARY PLATFORM]. Show how one piece of long-form content becomes: 5 tweets, 3 Instagram posts, 2 LinkedIn posts, 1 YouTube short, 1 TikTok, 1 email, and 1 blog excerpt. Include adaptation guidelines for each.

Hashtag Research Tool

Research and create hashtag sets for [NICHE/INDUSTRY] on Instagram. Create 5 sets of 30 hashtags each: broad reach (1M+), medium reach (100K-1M), niche (10K-100K), branded, and community. Explain strategy for mixing them.

Social Media Bio Optimizer

Write 10 variations of a social media bio for [BRAND/PERSON] on [PLATFORM]. Include different approaches: benefit-driven, personality-driven, credibility-driven, story-driven, and minimalist. Each optimized for the platform's character limit.

Community Management Guide

Create a community management guide for [BRAND] on [PLATFORMS]. Include: response time targets, tone of voice guide, response templates (20 scenarios), escalation process, proactive engagement routine, and community building tactics.

Social Media Audit Template

Create a comprehensive social media audit template. Evaluate: profile completeness, branding consistency, content performance, engagement metrics, follower growth, competitor comparison, posting consistency, and ROI. Include scoring system and action items.

Pinterest Strategy Builder

Create a Pinterest marketing strategy for [BUSINESS/BLOG]. Include: account optimization, board structure (10 boards), pin design templates, pinning frequency, keyword strategy, Rich Pins setup, Idea Pins plan, and traffic measurement.

YouTube Channel Strategy

Create a YouTube channel strategy for [NICHE]. Include: channel positioning, content pillars, video format types, upload schedule, thumbnail design guide, title/description SEO framework, community tab strategy, and monetization roadmap.

Social Media Holiday Calendar

Create a 3-month social media content calendar for [BRAND] including relevant holidays, awareness days, and industry events. For each, suggest: content type, angle, hashtag, and posting platform. Align with [BRAND VALUES/PRODUCTS].
