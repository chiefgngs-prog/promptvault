Free ChatGPT Prompts for Job Seekers

Searching for a job is a full-time job nobody pays you for. You tailor a resume for every posting, write a cover letter that does not sound like a template, prep for interviews that might cover anything, and then wait, often with no reply at all. It drains your confidence faster than your calendar. ChatGPT will not get you hired on its own, and recruiters can spot a raw AI cover letter from a mile away, but it is genuinely useful as a drafting partner. It can rework your bullet points to mirror a job description, generate practice questions for a specific role, run a mock interview with you, or help you phrase a sticky question about a gap or a layoff. The prompts below are built to produce a strong first draft you then edit in your own voice — never a finished letter you send untouched. Paste in your experience and the job posting, take the output, and make it yours.

13 Free Prompts

Resume Optimization Engine

Act as an expert resume writer. Optimize my resume for a [JOB TITLE] position at [COMPANY TYPE]. My current resume: [PASTE]. Improve: action verbs, quantified achievements (add metrics where possible), keyword optimization for ATS systems, bullet point structure (accomplishment + method + result), and visual scannability. Explain every change you make.

Cover Letter Customizer

Write a compelling cover letter for [JOB TITLE] at [COMPANY]. Use this job description: [PASTE]. Make it: 3 paragraphs only, opening with a hook (not 'I am writing to apply'), middle paragraph proving fit with 2 specific achievements, closing with enthusiasm and clear CTA. Match the company's tone from their website. No generic phrases.

Interview Question Predictor

I'm interviewing for [JOB TITLE] at [COMPANY]. Based on the job description [PASTE] and company context, predict the 15 most likely interview questions. Categorize: behavioral (STAR format), technical, situational, and culture-fit. For each question, provide what they're really evaluating and a framework for answering effectively.

STAR Story Builder

Help me build STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result) stories for interviews about [COMPETENCY - e.g., leadership, conflict resolution, overcoming failure]. Take my experience: [DESCRIBE]. Structure it into a 90-second story with: vivid Situation setup, clear Task, detailed Actions showcasing my skills, and quantified Results. Make it memorable.

Salary Negotiation Script

I received a job offer for [JOB TITLE] at [COMPANY]. The offer: [DETAILS]. Market rate: [RESEARCH]. Write negotiation scripts for: initial response (buy time), counter-offer conversation, handling pushback, negotiating non-salary benefits (if base is fixed), and accepting/rejecting gracefully. Include exact words and tone guidance.

LinkedIn Profile Optimizer

Optimize my LinkedIn profile for [TARGET ROLE]. Current headline: [PASTE]. Rewrite: headline (keyword-rich, value-proposing), about section (story-driven, not resume-repeating), experience section (achievement-focused), skills section (strategic), and featured section recommendations. Suggest 5 content ideas to boost visibility.

Career Change Roadmap

I want to transition from [CURRENT CAREER] to [TARGET CAREER]. Create a realistic roadmap: transferable skills audit, skill gaps to fill, certifications/education needed, portfolio projects to build, networking strategy, resume repositioning tips, timeline (3-6-12 months), and financial planning for the transition period.

Networking Conversation Guide

I'm attending [EVENT TYPE] and want to network effectively. Provide: pre-event preparation checklist, 10 conversation starters (not awkward), active listening techniques, graceful exit lines, LinkedIn follow-up templates (within 24 hours), relationship-nurturing cadence, and how to ask for a favor without being transactional.

Performance Review Preparer

Help me prepare for my annual performance review. I'm a [JOB TITLE]. Create: a self-assessment template, a system to document achievements with metrics, a way to frame challenges as growth, talking points for requesting a raise/promotion, responses to potential criticism, and a professional development plan to present.

Technical Interview Prep Plan

Create a 4-week technical interview prep plan for [ROLE - e.g., Software Engineer, Data Analyst]. Daily schedule covering: [TECH STACK/TYPE] practice, system design, behavioral prep, and company research. Include free resources, mock interview strategy, common question patterns, and a self-evaluation rubric.

Job Search Strategy Optimizer

My job search for [ROLE] has been [STRUGGLING/SLOW]. Audit my strategy: application volume vs quality, resume ATS optimization, networking activity, interview conversion rate, and follow-up consistency. Identify the bottleneck and create a focused 30-day action plan to improve results. Include weekly metrics to track.

Resignation Letter Writer

Help me resign from my [JOB TITLE] position at [COMPANY] professionally. Write a resignation letter that: states last day clearly, expresses gratitude genuinely, offers transition support, avoids negativity, and protects my reputation. Also provide: verbal resignation script, counter-offer response, and exit interview guidance.

Promotion Business Case

I want to make the case for my promotion from [CURRENT] to [TARGET]. Help me build a business case: document my achievements with quantified impact, list responsibilities I've taken on beyond my role, show market data for the target role, identify decision-makers, create a timeline for the conversation, and script the actual request.
